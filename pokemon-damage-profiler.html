👋 Welcome! Here's how to get started
1
Load your meta
Paste Pokémon sets from Pikalytics or pokepast.es into the Meta database below, then click Load meta. This is the list of Pokémon your team will face.
2
Add your Pokémon
Paste your Pokémon's set into the Your team box and click Add to tab(s). Add up to 6 for your full team.
3
See damage
The results panel shows how much damage each meta Pokémon deals to yours — and how much yours deals back. Click a result row to expand move details.
💡 Once you're comfortable, explore Type Synergies (🔗), Match tracking, and the Meta threat analysis in the results panel.
